Dear My Angrybird, 💗

Mujhe woh din aaj bhi yaad hai jab maine tujhe pehli baar bus mein dekha tha. Tu mere paas aakar baithi thi, aur sach bolun toh mujhe us waqt bahut achha laga tha. Usse pehle sab apna-apna crush bana rahe the, toh maine bhi mazaak mein Neha ko apna crush bol diya tha. Lekin us din pehli baar mujhe samajh aaya ki shayad pyaar feel kaisa hota hai. 💗

Mujhe woh sab yaad hai—bus ki left side ki third-last seat, hum dono ka wahan baithna, mera andar ki taraf baithna, aur tera mujhse poochhna, “Tu kal school aayega?” Chhoti si baat thi, par mere liye woh moment bahut special ban gaya. School se ghar aane wali woh bus ride meri sabse achhi yaadon mein se ek hai. Mujhe woh din aaj bhi bilkul clearly yaad hai. 🥹

Uske baad jab hum ghar pahunche, toh mujhse theek se khana bhi nahi khaya ja raha tha. Mere dimaag mein bas teri wahi baat ghoom rahi thi—“Tu kal school aayega?” Sach kahun, uske baad main roz bus mein tera intezaar karta tha, sochta tha ki tu kab aayegi.

Aur sabse funny baat pata hai? Tab mujhe tera naam tak nahi pata tha. Sach toh ye hai ki mujhe kisi bhi ladki ka naam nahi pata tha, phir bhi main roz tera wait karta tha. Bas tujhe ek baar phir dekhne ka mann karta tha. Aaj sochta hoon toh lagta hai, bina tera naam jaane hi tu meri roz ki sabse special umeed ban gayi thi. 💗

Shayad tujhe ye chhoti-chhoti baatein normal lagti hongi, lekin mere liye ye memories bahut special hain. Teri smile, tera gussa, teri baatein—ye sab mere din ko better bana dete hain.

Thank you meri life mein aane ke liye, My Angrybird. Tu mere liye bahut special hai. ❤️

Hamesha tere liye,
Aloo ♡
